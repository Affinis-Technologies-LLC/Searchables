# Contents

The clauses of the open document, as a tree, read from the document's own headings. Numbering is
kept as printed (`4.2.1`, `A.3`, appendix pages and all), so a clause number from a conversation
can be found by typing it in the filter.

- Choose a clause to go to its page.
- Filter by number or by words of the title.
- The tree is built by rules about headings, not by any model; a document with unusual headings
  may show a flatter tree. The [Library](library.md) view's health report says how well the
  document was read.
