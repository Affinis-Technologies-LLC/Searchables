# Compare

Two editions of a standard side by side: which clauses were added, removed or renumbered, which
changed their wording, and which changed their count of *shall*, *should* and *may*.

1. Choose the older and the newer edition.
2. The counts of clauses changed, added, removed and renumbered, and of requirement changes, are
   shown; **Only shall/should/may changes** keeps the clauses whose count of *shall*, *should* and
   *may* changed.
3. Choose a clause to see both texts with the differences marked. Tables are compared row by row.

The comparison matches clauses by number and title, then by text. A clause that moved and was
rewritten at the same time may show as removed and added; read those pairs together. The
[Catalogue](catalogue.md) shows the same changes from an identifier's side.
