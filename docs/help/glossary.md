# Glossary

The terms a document defines, and its acronyms, read from its definitions and acronyms clauses.

- Filter by the term or by words of the definition.
- Choose a term to open where it's defined.
- Acronyms found here are also used by [Search](search.md): searching for one finds its
  spelled-out form too.
- When a document has no definitions or acronyms clause in the usual form, the list is empty; the
  health report in the [Library](library.md) says which clauses were found.
